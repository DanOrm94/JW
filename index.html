01About Jamie

Hello Human

I am Jamie. An Integrative Well-being Coach & Mentor.

My approach is that we humans are multi-dimensional beings who are far more powerful than many of us realize.

Informed by neuroscience, yoga, somatic and nervous system awareness, I offer empowering words, somatic practices, personalised recorded reprogramming messages, and ongoing support to remind clients of what they already know deep inside of themselves.

I am here to ask some uncomfortable questions, to hold a mirror up for clients to become aware of their responsibility as an active participant and creator in their world.

This often looks like becoming more present daily, honoring and listening to the intuition, developing the physical and mental capacity to stay with uncomfortable emotions, creating discipline around how we show up and use our energy, embracing authenticity, and aligning our values with how we live.

I work with those who have already done some deep inner work and already understand themselves as an active creator of their lives and are willing to do the work to make the changes necessary to create the life they want.

04Qualifications

14 years of learning & practice

A lifelong curiosity about how humans thrive.

I have been immersing myself in the world of health, healing, and personal growth for 14 years. I started with an L3 Diploma in Personal Training in 2012, following a persistent curiosity to discover more about how humans thrive.

In 2017, I went on to complete a BSc (Hons) in Nutritional Science with a view to understanding how food, lifestyle, and environmental factors impact our well-being as humans.

I later began practicing Yoga, with curiosity about the mind-body connection. I still practice consistently and have a 500-hour TTC.

Most recently, in 2024, I began an MA, Master's in Transpersonal Psychology, Spirituality and Consciousness, which is ongoing with Liverpool John Moores and Alef Trust.
